# Executive findings

The deterministic illustrative data show treatment AOV of $96.56 versus $81.84 in matched control, a 17.98% observed difference. Core and Premium have a stronger illustrative pattern than Value. S06 in week 4 has 148 received rows versus 160 expected, so the output is not release-ready.

## Recommendations
1. Repair or formally exclude the incomplete feed and rerun vendor/control matching with confidence intervals and power.
2. If post-QA results are statistically and economically credible, extend only to Core and Premium with margin guardrails.
3. Redesign the Value offer before another test.
4. Require IT/vendor acceptance checks for row counts, unique IDs, treatment completeness, and metric definitions.

## Limitations
All data are synthetic and deterministic. Values do not represent Tailored Brands performance, customers, pricing, margin, Mastercard outputs, or causal conclusions. The pack does not model seasonality, spillover, pre-period trends, identity resolution, or actual vendor matching.
