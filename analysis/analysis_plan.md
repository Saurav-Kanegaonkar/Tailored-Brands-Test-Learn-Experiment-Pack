# Analysis plan

## Decision and design
Decide whether a targeted offer merits controlled expansion, revision, or stop. Four matched store cells are treatment and four are control for eight post-launch weeks. Primary outcome: net sales per transaction. Guardrails: margin, conversion, and feed completeness.

## Pre-readout gates
1. Confirm eligible-store balance by region and format.
2. Check assignment integrity and sample ratio by store/week.
3. Reconcile POS/CRM counts and null rates.
4. Review distributions, outliers, and segment patterns.
5. Estimate matched treatment-control differences overall and by segment.

## Statistical approach
Use vendor-approved control matching or matched-store difference-in-differences with pre-period adjustment. Report confidence intervals, power, practical significance, and regression diagnostics. This pack does not claim a production Mastercard Test & Learn run.
